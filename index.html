03 / Research process

How I explore each mix

01 — Understand

Identify composition, target properties, common applications, and the limitations of each concrete type.

02 — Interview

Ask practitioners how they choose, test, specify, place, and evaluate the material in practice.

03 — Document

Summarize key insights, cite sources, and distinguish interview experience from general technical background.

Sample questions for every interview

You can use these as your standard questionnaire.

  1. What is your role, and how do you work with concrete?
  2. When is this concrete type a better choice than conventional concrete?
  3. Which mix-design variables matter most in your experience?
  4. What testing or quality-control issues come up most often?
  5. What are the biggest placement or curing challenges?
  6. How do cost, durability, sustainability, and constructability affect your decisions?
  7. What misconception do students have about this material?
  8. What advice would you give an aspiring civil engineer?
